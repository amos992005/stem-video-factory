The pipeline requires every image and uses rembg u2netp and an alpha-mask component filter before sticker dilation; islands smaller than 5% of the largest component are discarded. This rule can also remove small disconnected intentional details. Props are fit and mathematically clamped within the requested stage; presenter painting is clipped to the lower zone. A head slice rotates around the neck while the torso bounces based on RMS; the horizontal split is approximate and can include raised hands. Backgrounds, headers, FX, and the subtitle pill are drawn in OpenCV, not procedural characters or props.

The inspected contact sheet shows all seven props, seven headers, large bottom-anchored presenters, no visible prop clipping or collisions, and no obvious floating white speck at the former locations. This is only a 16-frame sample, not proof of every frame or of artifact-free animated cutouts. Some compositions remain similar, and the lower-third pill obscures part of the presenter. No claim of a perfect 16-frame visual progression.

Engineering FX are schematic: 60Hz direction is a slowed analogy at 30 fps; the HUD avoids asserting that all chargers transform 120V to 5V AC, and does not implement the requested numerical countdown or claim pure 5V DC.
