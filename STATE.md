# AI comic cutout pilot

The previous raw SVG/emoji implementation was removed. The current pilot uses eight AI-generated PNG illustrations in `assets/comic_2d/`, generated with Arena's image tool (the model was not exposed; Flux/SDXL is not claimed). Four Dr. Volt poses use a common reference. The files are committed, so rerenders do not rely on image generation services. See the asset README for provenance. Entropy checks detect some invalid inputs but cannot establish semantic quality or prove AI origin.

[GitHub Actions run 45869416715](https://github.com/amos992005/stem-video-factory/actions/runs/45869416715) succeeded: 25.00 seconds, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 750 frames and an audio stream. Download `comic-cutout-pilot` from the run's Artifacts section (GitHub sign-in may be required; expiry 2026-12-26). The rendered MP4 is in the artifact rather than Git. The final build log, metadata, captions and 4×4 contact sheet are committed under `output/pilot/`.

The 68-word script was shortened with user approval. Edge Andrew +12% produced 23.89 seconds of naturally timed audio; end padding brought the output to 25 seconds. There was no extra tempo adjustment. The script says US wall power alternates at 60 Hz rather than presenting that frequency as universal. Faster-whisper word captions use MarginV=220; 68 events have zero temporal overlap. Ten tests passed. The full pipeline took 92.8s; drawing plus encoding took 45.6s, and the complete Actions job took 2m19s. Do not claim 800 frames in 15 seconds.

Actual implementation: rembg u2netp plus boundary-connected white key, OpenCV outline/shadow, audio-reactive whole-body squash/rock, 1.45-second composition changes, animated lights/particles, lightning, and cyan rings. SFX are mixed under narration. It does NOT implement every requested effect: there is no separate 60fps FX layer, timed 60Hz reversing-particle simulation, voltage gauge, animated diode gate, or true continuous whip-pan/speed-lines. Transitions are hard reframings with spring pops, and poses are whole-body cutouts rather than joint rigs. Sticker cutout validation does not ensure perfectly clean masks.

Contact sheet inspected: artwork and compositions improve markedly over emoji, but several cells remain similar, some subjects are small or partially cropped, and small white artifacts persist; 16 distinctly dynamic cells are not verified. Caption/character overlap seen on the first run was reduced. The generated transformer and other props are simplified analogies, not engineering schematics. The sandbox cannot download GitHub artifact storage, so the MP4's full playback and sound were not reviewed locally. No procedural character/prop fallback is used.
